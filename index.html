! Troubleshooting•18-sec video▶ Video IMPORTANT: Paper Tray and Blue Sensors Position the blue paper guides correctly so the copier detects the loaded paper size.
  1. 1

    Load the paper flat and square inside the tray.

  2. 2

    Move the blue side and end guides so they sit snugly against the paper stack.

  3. 3

    Do not leave a gap between the blue guides and the paper, and do not squeeze the paper until it bends.

  4. 4

    Close the tray and check the paper size shown on the copier screen.

  5. 5

    If the displayed size is wrong, reopen the tray and reposition the blue guides before continuing.

Important

The blue guides act as paper-size sensors. Incorrect guide placement can cause the copier to select the wrong paper size or display a mismatch message.

▣ Copy•2 min Reduce or enlarge a copy (Zoom) Shrink or enlarge with a preset, type your own percentage, or use Minimal so edges don’t get cut off.
Picture guideTap an image to enlarge
  1. 1

    Load your originals and open Copy.

  2. 2

    Tap Zoom. It shows 100.0% when copies will be the same size as the original.

  3. 3

    Tap a preset size, such as 64.7%, 77.2%, 78.5%, 121.4%, 129.4%, or 154.5%, or a Zoom Ratio button: 50.0%, 200.0%, or 400.0%.

  4. 4

    Need a different size? Tap the % box and type any percentage from 25.0% to 400.0% on the keypad, or use the – and + keys. Press C to go back to 100.0%.

  5. 5

    If the edges of your original are getting cut off, tap Minimal. It shrinks the image about 3% and centers it on the page.

  6. 6

    Tap OK, then press Start.

Trainer tip

When you type your own percentage, check the Preview before you print so you don’t waste a stack of copies at the wrong size.

▤ Print•2 min Sharper prints or toner saving from a PC (Quality tab) Choose photo, document, or CAD quality, 1200 dpi, Toner Save, and other print-quality settings.
Picture guideTap an image to enlarge
  1. 1

    Click File > Print > Properties, then open the Quality tab.

  2. 2

    Image Quality Setting: pick Document/Photo (the default), Document, Photo, or CAD.

  3. 3

    Resolution: keep 600 dpi (the default), or choose 1200 dpi for the best quality.

  4. 4

    Image Compression: High Compression (the default) prints faster. Best Quality takes a little longer to print.

  5. 5

    Edge Enhancement makes the edges of text and lines stand out: Weak (the default), Medium, or Strong.

  6. 6

    Click OK, then Print.

Trainer tip

Leave the defaults for everyday printing. Change them to match what you’re printing, like photos, CAD drawings, or fine lines.
