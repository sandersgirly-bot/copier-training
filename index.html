▣ Copy•1 min Make a basic copy Choose color, quantity, paper, and start a clean copy job.
  1. 1

    Place originals face up in the document feeder, or face down on the glass.

  2. 2

    Tap Copy on the Home screen.

  3. 3

  4. 4

    Enter the number of sets using the keypad.

  5. 5

▣ Copy•2 min Copy two-sided Turn one- or two-sided originals into a two-sided set.

▣ Copy•20-sec video▶ Video IMPORTANT: Make Sure Sensors Are Adjusted to Paper Size You never want to see question marks on the screen.
  1. 1

  2. 2

! Troubleshooting•18-sec video▶ Video IMPORTANT: Paper Tray and Blue Sensors Position the blue paper guides correctly so the copier detects the loaded paper size.
  1. 1

    Load the paper flat and square inside the tray.

  2. 2

    Move the blue side and end guides so they sit snugly against the paper stack.

  3. 3

    Do not leave a gap between the blue guides and the paper, and do not squeeze the paper until it bends.

  4. 4

    Close the tray and check the paper size shown on the copier screen.

  5. 5

    If the displayed size is wrong, reopen the tray and reposition the blue guides before continuing.

Important

The blue guides act as paper-size sensors. Incorrect guide placement can cause the copier to select the wrong paper size or display a mismatch message.

⌁ Scan•2 min Scan to email Send a clean PDF directly from the copier.
  1. 1

    Load originals and tap Scan/Fax on the Home screen.

  2. 2

    Select a saved email address or tap Direct Input to type one.

  3. 3

    Choose PDF as the file type and 300 dpi for most office documents.

  4. 4

    Add a subject or file name if needed.

  5. 5

    Press Start, then verify the scan arrives before leaving the device.

Trainer tip

Use Compact PDF for a smaller attachment; use higher resolution for fine print or photos.

  3. 3

  4. 4

! Troubleshooting•3 min Clear a paper jam safely Follow the animated guide and avoid torn paper inside the device.
  1. 1

    Read the screen to identify the highlighted door or tray.

  2. 2

    Open only the areas shown in the animated guide.

  3. 3

    Turn green knobs and move green levers in the indicated direction.

  4. 4

    Pull paper slowly with both hands and remove every torn piece.

  5. 5

    Close each door firmly and confirm the message clears.

Important

Do not touch labeled hot areas in the fusing section. Call support if paper tears where you cannot safely reach it.

! Troubleshooting•2 min Fix a paper-size mismatch Resolve prompts when the device expects a different paper size or type.
  1. 1

    Check the size and type requested in the on-screen message.

  2. 2

    Confirm the paper in the selected tray matches that request.

  3. 3

    Move the tray guides snugly against the paper.

  4. 4

    Confirm the paper size and type on screen after closing the tray.

  5. 5

    If appropriate, select a different tray and continue the job.

Trainer tip

Heavy, label, transparency, and letterhead settings affect temperature and speed—do not label them as Plain paper.
